Live Video & Audio Calls

Start a private call for a support session with a counsellor, or a group call with your peer circle — no app download required.

Calls run on the free, open Jitsi Meet platform, encrypted in transit. For a scheduled 1:1 with a professional, please book a Theraphy Session so a counsellor can meet you at the right time.

Share this room name (or the link below) with the person or group you want to talk to. Anyone with it can join.

Tip

Before you join

Find a private, well-lit spot. Test your camera and mic. Headphones help keep the conversation confidential.

Safety

Keep it respectful

Calls are unmoderated peer-to-peer spaces. If a call turns unsafe, leave immediately and use the helpline above.
